# Anti-slop audit 001: Personalize > Layout (2026-11-17)

Scope: the new Layout options in Settings > Look and Feel > Personalize (`src/browser/components/preferences/customPersonalize.inc.xhtml`, `src/zen/custom/custom-settings.js`), the look they change in the browser (`src/zen/custom/custom-look.css`), and the new defaults (`prefs/custom/look.yaml`, bookmarks bar always, window buttons always shown).

Mode: after (chosen by the owner for this session). Nothing below has been fixed yet. Pick the numbers to fix.

Direction used: there is no `DESIGN.md`. The direction came from the owner's answers in chat: friendlier for everyone, softer and bigger, names on buttons, each person chooses. Design Read: settings and browser chrome for everyday users, in Zen's existing visual language, dial ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1.

## Hard Gate (checked, no finding)

- R-02: no em dash in the new text (`custom-preferences.ftl`).
- R-26 / R-35: every control was clicked through in a built test window. Size Large: buttons 38px; Compact: 30px. Corners Extra round: radius 22px, and back to 14px. Light or dark: the theme pref changes. Tabs sidebar Right: the sidebar moves to x=1025, and back to x=0. Bookmarks bar Always: 34px bar on web pages. Button names off: Library button 32px wide; on: 76px. Window buttons off: top bar 8px; on: 34px. Changing a pref elsewhere updates the dropdown. No console errors.
- R-32: native XUL dropdowns and checkboxes, reachable by keyboard; every dropdown has a `<label control=...>`.
- R-34: light and dark both render. Light was checked in the capture.
- R-17 / R-18 / R-36 / R-38: no numbers, claims or made-up content.
